Seven stretches, five minutes

Each one on the poster includes what you should feel and the mistake to avoid, so you know you're doing it right.

1

Ear-to-Shoulder Stretch

Sit tall, gently tilt your head to bring ear toward shoulder. Hold 20–30 seconds, then switch sides.

You should feelA gentle stretch along the opposite side of the neck.
Common mistakeShrugging the shoulder up — keep it relaxed and down.
2

Chin Tuck

Gently tuck your chin back as if making a double chin. Hold 5–10 seconds, repeat 5–8 times.

You should feelA stretch along the back of the neck and better posture.
Common mistakeTucking chin down toward the chest — keep it level, move straight back.
3

Slow Neck Turns

Slowly turn your head over one shoulder, hold 15–20 seconds, then repeat on the other side.

You should feelA stretch along the side and back of the neck.
Common mistakeTurning too quickly — move slowly and smoothly.
4

Neck Flexion Stretch

Lower your chin slowly toward your chest, with gentle hand support if needed. Hold 20–30 seconds.

You should feelA stretch along the back of the neck and upper back.
Common mistakeRounding the shoulders forward — keep them relaxed and down.
5

Shoulder Rolls

Roll shoulders backward in slow circles 5–8 times, then forward 5–8 times, arms relaxed.

You should feelLoosening and relaxation in the shoulders and upper back.
Common mistakeRolling too fast — keep movements slow and controlled.
6

Upper Trap Release

Reach over your head, gently pull it toward the opposite side. Hold 20–30 seconds, then switch.

You should feelA stretch along the shoulder and upper neck area.
Common mistakeLifting the opposite shoulder — keep it down and relaxed.
7

Levator Scapulae Stretch

Turn your head about 45°, then gently look down toward your armpit. Hold 20–30 seconds, then switch sides.

You should feelA stretch from the side of the neck down to the shoulder blade.
Common mistakePulling straight down — turn your head diagonally first, then look down.

Safety tips

  • Stop if you feel sharp pain, tingling, or dizziness.
  • Move slowly — no bouncing or jerking.
  • Breathe normally and steadily throughout.
  • Only stretch to gentle comfort, never to pain.
